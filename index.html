Who am I

My name is Su Tung Loc (Tony), I am 24 years old, and I am an international student from Vietnam. I recently graduated with a Master's degree in computer science from the University of New Mexico, where I also completed my Bachelor's degree in the same field.

While pursuing my Master's degree, I focused on some specific fields:

  • Machine Learning
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • HPC
  • Software Engineering

Additional to my academic journey, I also want to involve myself in more social work. Coming from a developing country like Vietnam, I have witnessed firsthand the challenges that many unfortunate families face, particularly in rural areas where education, healthcare, and basic necessities are limited. Before I came to the U.S. to study, during every summer, my mother and I regularly joined different social work events to travel to less fortunate areas to contribute in ways that can make a tangible difference. This inspired me to potentially apply my academic knowledge in different ways to be part of efforts that can uplift, empower, and leave meaningful impacts on those who need it most.

I am currently under OPT, so I do not need a sponsorship. You can visit my CV to see my resume, research papers, and more of my interests.
